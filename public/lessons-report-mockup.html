Signal
Lessons are compounding — July set the record
391 lessons and $57,246 in July 2026, the best month ever and +62% over July 2025. The trendline has grown through both seasons.
Signal
This is a loyalty business
84% of all lessons come from repeat students; 816 students have booked 2+, and the most devoted booked 72. Retention, not acquisition, drives revenue.
Opportunity
Pickleball coaching is barely tapped
81 pickleball lessons vs 4,831 tennis — yet pickleball open-play blocks are among the busiest court holds. Recruiting 2–3 pickleball pros meets proven demand.
Risk
1 in 9 lessons cancels
524 canceled bookings and $70,682 refunded all-time. Worth checking whether cancellations cluster by instructor, weather, or lead time.

📋 Data notes
Lessons live in the programs pipeline: the marketplace publishes one section per lesson slot, and a booking is a program registration with an instructor attached. Figures here come from the instructor registrations feed (booking grain, signup-date basis) and the programs feed (slot supply). Court time consumed by lessons is not yet part of the facility/court-utilization feeds — that join is the proposed Metabase card extension. Revenue is participant payments; instructor compensation splits live in the Instructor Payout report.
